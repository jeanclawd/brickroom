Opus 5.5 Brickworks

What people are building with Claude Opus 5.5 across three threads: physical LEGO designs with real instructions, brick-style films, and 3D, voxel and isometric games that run in the browser.

Summary

LEGO you can actually buildOpus 5.5 outputs part lists and step-by-step booklets, and it checks collisions, connections and balance. It also orders the parts on BrickLink.
Brick films from a photoA community skill turns one photo into a Three.js diorama. The diorama builds itself brick by brick, then animates as stop-motion.
Games in one sessionPeople are cloning Fortnite, Minecraft and Terraria in Three.js in about 3 hours each, and building playable isometric pixel dioramas.

Gap: I found no public project that chains a LEGO model into an isometric or 2.5D game. All the parts exist separately (LDraw generation, a brick renderer, isometric scenes). The pipeline at the end shows how they could fit together.

LEGO assemblies

Life-size Microduck from real parts

Victor M (@victormustar) on X, ~24 Sep

He asked Opus 5.5 to design a Microduck he could build with real LEGO. The model designed it life-size and validated the build. It then produced a LEGO-style instruction booklet, priced every piece in the browser and prepared the orders on BrickLink from 3 sellers.

Anthropic's own demo: a toy brick-building app

@claudeai on X, launch week

"A toy brick-building app. Turn a photo or a description into a buildable model with custom instructions." This is one of the official early-exploration clips for Opus 5.5. Linus Ekenstam's reaction to the LEGO demos: LEGO should ship an official bricks API so you could order anything you design.

BrickBuilderAI: direct Claude to LDraw

jjohnson5253/brickbuilderai · PR #127 (open source)

A new /claudeToBricks endpoint sends text or images straight to claude-opus-5-5 and gets back an LDraw model. The output is validated, stored with a parts list, converted to MPD and rendered in 3D with instructions.

  • Opus 5.5 does not support forced tool choice. The endpoint accepts either structured tool output or a plain-text LDR fallback, and validates both.
  • Flat price of $0.10 per piece for Claude-direct generations. Model override: ANTHROPIC_LDR_MODEL.

Tooling people pair it with

  • LDraw (.ldr/.mpd): the open part library and file format. The model writes it as text.
  • LPub3D and Web Lic: turn LDraw files into instruction booklets (PDF or images).
  • BrickLink: part pricing and multi-seller ordering.

Videos and animation

brick-film skill (HyperFrames)

heygen-com/hyperframes-community-skills · PR #17 · Opus 5.5 is credited as co-author

You give it one photo. It builds a Three.js diorama from real brick shapes: bricks, plates, tiles, slopes, and minifigs with printed faces and molded hair. The diorama assembles itself brick by brick until it matches the photo's framing. It then plays as a stop-motion brickfilm animated on twos. A label counts parts and stud connections as they land.

  • Pipeline: new-project.sh scaffolds → trace_object.py maps the image to a stud × plate grid → shoot.sh matches the camera over several rounds → verify.sh checks determinism → local GPU render.
  • Kit: ES modules for part geometry, a voxel-to-brick converter, a minifig rig and car builders. Its only dependency is Three.js.
  • Limits: renders locally only. Pins Three.js 0.181.2, GSAP 3.14.2 and HyperFrames CLI 0.8.74.

Other code-generated films

  • Michael Guo: a 2-minute sand-animation film of US history, with music and sound effects.

3D, voxel and isometric games

Isometric: The Cyclops' Island

neta.art · "Opus 5.5 Pixel Art Scenes Creator"

This is the clearest isometric 2.5D example. It is a playable diorama of Odyssey Book IX: a shore with a camp and a ship, a dark grove, and Polyphemus's cave. You move with WASD or click-to-walk, and the climax has dialogue choices. It sits next to eight non-interactive lo-fi loop scenes.

Voxel and block worlds

  • VoxelCraft (eRebernisek, GitHub): infinite terrain, biomes, caves and a day/night cycle. Plain ES modules with no build step. Textures and sounds are procedural, with no external assets.
  • Lumen Vale (Noah Wachnik): a Minecraft-style world with custom shaders and water ripples.
  • ChrisGPT: a block world with brick houses, streetlights and realistic cloud lighting.
  • bluedev: Opus 5.5 built a voxel self-portrait of itself, animated.
  • Vib3Coded, "Pelagia": an island railway with an underwater cross-section. The style is close to a tilt-shift diorama.
  • nicekate: an IKEA-style step-by-step furniture assembly animation. The instruction-booklet idea works outside LEGO too.

What these projects have in common

  • Everything is code. Most projects are one Three.js file or a few ES modules, with procedural geometry, textures and audio and no asset pipeline.
  • Built-in verification. The best LEGO work checks its own output: collision tests, connection counts, centre of mass and a "buildable at every step" check. The brick-film skill checks determinism and camera match.
  • Lighting matters most. Reviewers credit lighting and colour grading for the look. Characters and map pieces still look unfinished close up.
  • Limits. Expect lag and poor optimisation in one-shot games. Cost adds up fast. One creator used 15% of a weekly allowance in a single session (reported for Opus 5).

A possible pipeline: LEGO set to isometric game

Nobody has published this chain yet. Each step reuses something from above:

  1. Photo or prompt → Opus 5.5 → LDraw .ldr. Validate collisions and connectivity, as BrickBuilderAI and the Microduck project do.
  2. LDraw → LPub3D → a PDF booklet, plus a BrickLink parts list for the physical build.
  3. The same model → the brick-film kit's part geometry → a build-up animation, then a stop-motion video.
  4. The same Three.js scene with an OrthographicCamera at the true isometric angle (35.264° pitch, 45° yaw) → a 2.5D game level. Minifigs become sprites or rigged characters, and stud positions become the walk grid.

One source model gives you a buildable set, a video and a playable level. The stud grid gives the game discrete tiles for free.
